# Get help

<!-- REQUIRED page. -->

- **In class:** ask your instructor. Stuck for five minutes means ask; nobody is graded on getting stuck.
- **Your login doesn't work:** your instructor can reset it. Don't keep retrying: three wrong tries locks the account for a while.
- **After class:** the lab team at lab@paragonmicro.com, or your Paragon Micro account team.

This site is public and never contains passwords or anything about your company's environment.
